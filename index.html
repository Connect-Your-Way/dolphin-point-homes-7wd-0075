Who we are

A local builder for a considered build.

Dolphin Point Homes is a licensed NSW builder working across the Ulladulla and Shoalhaven coast. We take on new homes, renovations, extensions and commercial projects — the kind of work where the details matter and the build has to last in a coastal environment. Every project is planned carefully, communicated clearly, and finished properly.

01Our approach

Built well, built to last by the sea.

Coastal building asks more of a home — salt air, weather, and the way light and space are used. We build with materials and methods chosen for the conditions, and we keep you informed at every stage so there are no surprises.
02Sustainable & accessible

Comfortable, efficient, adaptable homes.

We favour sustainable, energy-efficient design and homes that are easy to move around and adapt over time — comfortable to live in now and into the future.
