About us

A builder rooted in the Shoalhaven.

Dolphin Point Homes is a licensed NSW builder serving the Ulladulla and Shoalhaven coast. We take on new homes, renovations and extensions, commercial projects and modular builds — practical, well-considered work suited to living on the South Coast.

Coastal building asks more of a home. Salt air, weather and the way light and space are used all matter, so we build with materials and methods chosen for the conditions and keep the process clear from first drawings to handover.

Across everything we do, the priorities stay the same: careful planning, clear communication, quality workmanship, and sustainable, adaptable design that keeps up with how you live.

How we work

The things that don't change from job to job.

Careful planning

Every project is thought through before it starts — the site, the coastal conditions and the way you want to live all shape the plan.

Clear communication

You are kept informed at each stage, so the build is easy to follow and there are no surprises along the way.
